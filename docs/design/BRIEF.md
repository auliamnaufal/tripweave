# Tripweave UI brief

Written under explicit creative delegation ("go as creative as a professional UI/UX designer"). Data and photos are placeholders; the functional planner still lives at `/lab`.

## Inputs

- References: dark-forest editorial travel site (word-by-word manifesto with floating photos, torn-paper edge, monochrome trip cards), a light green tour site (sticky process steps, valley-shaped CTA), a cinematic Japan destination switcher (huge city name, native script, red sun disc), and an AI travel chat workspace (sidebar, chat, destination panel, flight booking).
- Product: an agent plans flights, a bed and the days inside a hard budget; the traveller pays once with PayPal; the agent then books each supplier.

## Direction

- Vibe: quiet expedition journal. Calm, tactile, a little cinematic.
- Palette: deep forest greens and warm paper, one ember accent taken from the hiker's jacket and the Kyoto sun.
- Type: Instrument Serif for display, Geist for text, Geist Mono for small data.
- Imagery: one photographic world (Portra film look), generated with OpenAI gpt-image-2.6 from one shared style preamble.

## Journey (landing)

1. Recognition: a layered dusk valley, the headline sits between the far mountains and the hiker's rock.
2. Turn: a manifesto lights up word by word while photos drift past.
3. Range: monochrome journey cards that wake into colour; one opens into a full-screen sheet.
4. Desire: a pinned cinematic switcher, one city per wipe.
5. Substance: four honest steps, a sticky photo stack.
6. Peak: "One plan. One payment." Scattered bookings are pulled onto one thread and tied into a single PayPal ticket, under budget.
7. Proof: the workspace itself, tilted columns drifting in opposite directions.
8. Close: the valley opens and asks "Where to next?" with a real prompt field.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | awe, stillness | depth that moves at different speeds under your hand |
| Manifesto | recognition | your own reason for travelling, lit one word at a time |
| Journeys | curiosity | grey cards that bloom into colour when touched |
| Cities | longing | a full-bleed city wipes in, then another |
| Steps | trust | plain language, one step at a time |
| The weave (peak) | relief | chaos of bookings tied into one total, under budget |
| Workspace | confidence | the actual tool, calm and legible |
| Close | invitation | the valley opens, a cursor waits in the field |

Peak, as a visitor would say it: "All the bookings literally get stitched into one ticket and you pay once."

Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where your whole trip gets woven into one payment."

Signature move: the ember thread. It draws itself through the peak section, loops around each booking card and pulls them into one ticket. The same thread reappears in the trip page timeline.
